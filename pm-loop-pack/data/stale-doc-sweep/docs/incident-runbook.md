# Incident Runbook

Last updated: 2026-01-12 · Owner: Dana Okafor (Support)

## Declaring an incident
Anyone can declare. Post in #incidents with severity (SEV1-SEV3) and a one-line summary. On-call engineer becomes incident commander.

## Comms cadence
- SEV1: status page update every 30 minutes.
- SEV2: internal updates hourly; status page if customer-visible.

## Postmortems
Blameless postmortem within 5 business days for SEV1/SEV2. Template in the postmortems folder.

## After the incident
Support sends follow-ups to affected accounts from the approved template. Legal reviews anything touching data exposure.
