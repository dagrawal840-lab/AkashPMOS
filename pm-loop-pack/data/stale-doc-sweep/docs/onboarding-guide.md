# New Customer Onboarding Guide

Last updated: 2026-02-10 · Owner: Priya Raman (CS)

Welcome kit for CSMs running kickoff calls with new accounts.

## Step 1 — Plan selection
Most mid-market teams should start on the **Growth plan at $49/user/month**, which includes unlimited Inbox Rules and SLA policies. Upsell to Enterprise only if they need SSO or audit logs.

## Step 2 — Workspace setup
Create the workspace, invite agents, connect the support email address. Aim to complete within the first call.

## Step 3 — First-week check-in
Confirm the team has created at least 3 Inbox Rules and one SLA policy. Share the reporting guide.

## Escalations
Ping #cs-escalations in Slack for anything blocking go-live.
