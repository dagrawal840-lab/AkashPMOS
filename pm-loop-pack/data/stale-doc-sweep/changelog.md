# Brightline — Product Changelog

> Rolling log of shipped changes. Owned by PM team. Newest first.

## 2026-07-08 — Slack alerts for SLA breaches
Added optional Slack notifications when a ticket breaches its SLA target. Configured under Settings > Notifications.

## 2026-06-30 — SSO available on Pro plan
SAML SSO, previously Enterprise/Scale-only, is now included in the Pro plan. Okta and Entra ID connectors GA.

## 2026-06-12 — "Inbox Rules" renamed to "Automations"
The Inbox Rules feature is now called Automations. New builder UI, same trigger/condition/action model. Old deep links redirect until 2026-09-01.

## 2026-05-27 — Bulk ticket merge
Agents can merge up to 20 tickets at once from list view.

## 2026-05-06 — New pricing plans (Growth plan retired)
Replaced the Starter/Growth/Enterprise lineup with **Starter ($29/user/mo), Pro ($59/user/mo), Scale ($119/user/mo)**. The **Growth plan ($49/user/mo) is deprecated**; existing Growth customers were migrated to Pro at grandfathered pricing through 2026-12-31. See data/stale-doc-sweep/pricing-current.md.

## 2026-04-28 — Reports CSV export removed
Removed the "Export CSV" button from the Reports page (performance issues on large workspaces). Replaced by **scheduled email exports** (Settings > Reports > Scheduled exports).

## 2026-04-20 — Mobile app dark mode
iOS and Android apps follow system theme.
