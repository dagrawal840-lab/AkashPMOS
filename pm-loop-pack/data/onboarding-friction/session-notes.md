# Onboarding session observation notes

**Author:** Priya Raman (UX Research)
**Last updated: 2026-06-03**
**Sessions:** 6 moderated new-user sessions, recorded 2026-05-26 to 2026-06-02

> NOTE: These notes predate the connect-wizard changes shipped in late June.
> Treat as historical context, not current-state evidence.

## Session summaries

- **S1 (ops manager, logistics, 2026-05-26):** Breezed through account + workspace. Paused 90s at connect step deciding between Postgres and "generic SQL". Completed.
- **S2 (data analyst, fintech, 2026-05-27):** Skipped invite-team ("I'll add people once there's something to look at"). This matches what we hear constantly — solo evaluation first, invite later.
- **S3 (founder, e-commerce, 2026-05-28):** Failed email verification on first try (corporate spam filter). Recovered via resend.
- **S4 (PM, healthtech, 2026-05-29):** Skipped invite-team as well. Called it "a later thing." Completed dashboard with template.
- **S5 (engineer, SaaS, 2026-06-01):** Fastest session (7 min end to end). Wanted a connection-string paste option at connect step.
- **S6 (marketer, agency, 2026-06-02):** Confused by dashboard template names, not by setup.

## Themes

1. Invite-team skipping is intentional user behavior, not friction — 2 of 6 skipped deliberately with clear rationale.
2. Connect step is the highest-effort step but was completing fine as of these sessions.
3. Email verification issues are environmental (spam filters), not product.
