## Hatchboard Plans

**Free** — $0
Unlimited 1:1 bookings, 1 calendar connection, Hatchboard branding.

**Pro** — $12/user/mo
Everything in Free, plus: group bookings, 6 calendar connections, custom branding, **Auto-Reschedule (new)**, availability polls.

**Business** — $28/user/mo
Everything in Pro, plus: round-robin routing, SSO/SAML, audit log, priority support.

Footnote: Auto-Reschedule proposes new times when conflicts appear; included in Pro and Business at no extra charge.

Confirmed with Finance (R. Iyer) 2026-07-11: no SKU or tier change for this launch.
