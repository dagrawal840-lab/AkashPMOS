# Support macros — Auto-Reschedule (Zendesk)

Owner: Sam Reyes · Published to Zendesk 2026-07-14 (macro IDs 4417–4420)

## Macro 4417 — "What is Auto-Reschedule?"
Hi {{ticket.requester.first_name}}, Auto-Reschedule is available on Pro and above. When a conflict appears on a booking, Hatchboard proposes up to 3 new times to everyone invited and books the first one all parties accept. Enable it under Settings → Scheduling → Auto-Reschedule.

## Macro 4418 — "Attendee didn't get suggestions"
Ask for the booking ID. Check the attendee's notification preferences — suggestions go to email only in v1 (no SMS). If prefs look right, escalate to #eng-scheduling with the booking ID.

## Macro 4419 — "How do I turn it off?"
Settings → Scheduling → toggle off Auto-Reschedule. Pending suggestion sets are withdrawn immediately.

## Macro 4420 — "Wrong time proposed"
Confirm the host's working hours and timezone in Settings. Known edge: hosts with overlapping calendars connected may see slots from the secondary calendar. Workaround: disconnect and reconnect the primary.
