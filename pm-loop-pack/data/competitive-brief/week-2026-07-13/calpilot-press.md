## CalPilot announces new Pro pricing and a 3-seat minimum — July 9, 2026
Effective August 1, 2026, the Pro plan moves from $18 to $24 per user per month (annual) and $29 month-to-month, with a new 3-seat minimum. Existing Pro customers keep $18 pricing through their current term, then renew at the new rate. The Free plan drops from 3 event types to 2. CFO Elena Ruiz: "Pro now carries routing forms and higher pool caps — the price reflects the packaging."

## CalPilot named a "Strong Performer" in Forrester Wave: Scheduling Automation, Q3 2026 — July 1, 2026
CalPilot, Inc. today announced it was named a Strong Performer, citing top scores in the enterprise administration and routing criteria.
