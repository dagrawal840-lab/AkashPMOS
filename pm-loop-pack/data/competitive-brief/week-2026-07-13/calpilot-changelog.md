# CalPilot Product Updates
<!-- snapshot captured 2026-07-13 07:00 PT · source: calpilot.io/updates -->

## Week of July 6, 2026
- **Plans update.** Alongside the new Pro pricing (see announcement, July 9), the Pro plan now includes routing forms (previously Enterprise-only) and raises the round-robin pool cap from 10 to 25 members.
- SCIM provisioning is out of beta on Enterprise.
- Bug fix: routing forms with Clearbit branching mis-routed EU visitors when enrichment timed out.

## Week of June 29, 2026
- Routing forms now support conditional branching on company size (pulled from Clearbit enrichment).
- Round-robin pools can weight members by close rate imported from Salesforce.
- Bug fix: ICS attachments were malformed for events longer than 24 hours.
