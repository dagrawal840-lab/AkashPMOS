# User interview — Fernwood Supply Co.
**Date:** 2026-07-16 · **Interviewer:** Diego Fuentes (AE, sat in) + Priya Raman (PM) · **Recorded:** no (participant declined), notes only
**Participant:** June Calloway, Office Manager · Starter plan, signed up 2026-07-06

## Context
25 min. New-customer interview from the day-10 cohort. Regional building-supply distributor, 35 employees, no IT staff.

## Onboarding section
Setup took her "the better part of a week." Quote: "The wizard kept asking me things I didn't have answers to — what's an SSO? I just wanted to load our customer list and go." Abandoned the wizard on day one, came back after a support chat walked her through CSV import. Quote: "If Marcus in support hadn't screen-shared with me, I would have asked for a refund."
Suggested a "small business path" that skips the integrations and SSO steps entirely.

## Dashboard section
Now that she's set up, daily use is fine, but: "First thing in the morning the dashboard takes a good 20, 25 seconds. I make the coffee run while it loads." Not a dealbreaker at her size, "just embarrassing when I demo it to the owner."

## Wrap-up
Overall glad she stayed. Would recommend "with a warning about week one."
